## 4. Interpretation for Paper Writing

- Exp4 evaluates whether objective settings transfer cleanly across multiple task scenarios.
- The primary quantity of interest is `scenario_primary_value`, which changes meaning by scenario.
- `scenario_score` is the scenario-aware scalar used for within-scenario ranking, while `composite_score` remains the default balanced score.
- For line-theme scenarios, interpret results mainly through `line_theme_ratio` / scenario satisfaction rather than the balanced composite.
