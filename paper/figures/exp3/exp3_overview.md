# Exp3 Overview (Supplement to Exp1)

## Key Findings

- Experiment 3 should be framed as a supplement to Experiment 1, focusing on whether the methods remain close to the score-time ideal point.
- Task-level balance leader: `Ours (Full)` (average ideal-point distance `0.214`).
- Query-level closest to the quality-speed ideal point: `Ours (Full)` (distance `0.167`).
- `Ours (Full)` vs `NSGA-II` on task-level: `358` higher-score-and-faster tasks, `10` higher-score-but-slower tasks, `218` lower-score-but-faster tasks.
- Query-level summary: `Ours (Full)` has `82` higher-score-and-faster queries.
